## Artwork and manufacture

Palette: purple #7C4DFF; lilac #B9A0FF; midnight #120A24; bone #F3F0E9; mint #65D6C4; peach #F1A77A. These are screen colours, not physical ink recipes. Matte white vinyl with printed colour is the proposed starting stock; neither weather resistance nor dishwasher resistance is claimed without supplier testing.

Use Manrope 700/800 and Newsreader 500. Fonts are live text and not embedded. Install these exact families, check font metrics and text bounds at final size, then outline a production copy while keeping editable originals. The original wordmark file is embedded unchanged, but its transparent padding and raster resolution require a proper vector original before final label manufacture. No recreated wordmark is approved by the generated mockup.

Printers should add their required bleed (commonly 2–3mm, confirm), safety margin and named spot-colour cut path. These files do NOT contain production cut paths or bleed. The isolated F has disconnected parts: use one enclosing kiss-cut silhouette so its detached bar stays with the F. The contour lettering design likewise needs a connected backing shape, not separate loose letters. Round/rectangle/oval backgrounds show intended trim geometry only. Do not send the mockup photograph as print artwork.

For economy, quote a digitally printed kiss-cut sheet of the full set and a smaller individually cut selection. Actual price depends on quantity, stock, finish, cut complexity and printer. Avoid adding foil/holographic effects to this first matte collection. Check small lettering and serif hairlines in a printed proof.

Recommended first pack: 01 bone editorial, 03 lilac F, 05 peach company, 06 midnight credits, 10 purple strip, 12 After Hours oval. The large serif 01 is strongest for the social slogan; 06 carries the existing merchandise collection; 03 makes the app symbol recognisable.
