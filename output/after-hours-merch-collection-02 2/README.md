# Flixie After Hours — collection 02

A premium cinema-club wardrobe using the approved softened F, the preserved original wordmark, and the updated colour/type plan. Two AI-generated concept lookbooks sit alongside eight editable, dimensioned SVG artwork files. Mockups are not manufacturing proofs. Any extra wording or garment labels invented in the generated photographs are not approved artwork.

## Recommended launch capsule

1. Midnight heavyweight tee: 32mm-wide lilac F on wearer’s left chest, plain back. Start print top approximately 80–100mm below shoulder seam, 80–100mm left of centre; size-grade on the chosen blank. One ink/position is the economical option; embroidery raises unit cost and needs backing on jersey. Target blank 220–260gsm, subject to supplier sampling.
2. Midnight unstructured cap: bone F, 28mm high, centred front, bottom about 15mm above visor seam. One thread colour; approve digitising and sew-out. No fine serif lettering.
3. Washed charcoal credits hoodie: 280 × 145mm back layout, print top about 110–140mm below back-neck seam so the hood does not cover the headline. Bone Manrope 800 plus lilac F. Optional 32mm front F adds a second decoration position. Target blank 350–450gsm; verify actual hand-feel and print compatibility.
4. Bone canvas tote: editorial type in Newsreader 500, 240 × 152mm design, centred and approximately 90mm below top edge on a suggested 380 × 420mm bag. Choose the one-ink midnight SVG for launch. Peach underline edition adds a second spot colour in the same position. Midnight F deliberately replaces the mockup’s lilac F to reduce this variant from three inks to two.

## Expansion pieces

- Editorial bone tee: reuse the tote artwork at 100% or up to 117% (281mm wide), centred back. Optional lilac chest F is a separate position. Keep serif text large.
- Social statement tee: 280 × 315mm back design, lilac F plus mint Manrope 700 line. Place top about 80–100mm below back-neck seam. Plain front. Two colours in one position; large ink coverage may feel heavy, so sample the printer’s soft-hand ink system. Stronger visual statement, less versatile than the chest F tee.
- Mint F sticker: 50mm circle. Peach After Hours sticker: 80 × 25mm rounded rectangle. SVGs show finished trim size; printer must add bleed/cut paths to its template. Do not treat the visible artwork boundary as a supplied cutting path.

Placement dimensions are starting specifications, not one-size-fits-all patterns. Adjust to blank construction, garment size, hood depth and cap panel seams. Mockup scale is illustrative.

Neutral blanks dominate, purple-family details identify Flixie, mint or peach supports a particular piece. Do not add all accent colours to each garment. No gradients in print art. HEX is not a physical ink/thread recipe; match approved physical drawdowns/thread cards rather than inventing Pantone equivalents. Dark blanks may require an underbase, adding a screen and cost even where the design has one visible colour.

Manrope 700/800 is the merchandise sans serif; Newsreader 500 is the editorial serif. SVG lettering is live and editable, with no font binaries embedded. Install the correct licensed families before editing/exporting; preserve this live-text source and create outlined production copies after approval. Manrope is already in the app assets; Newsreader is available from its official repository: https://github.com/productiontype/Newsreader . Do not substitute the generated mockup’s condensed hoodie lettering for the specified Manrope. Do not type a replacement Flixie wordmark.

## Production status

- F-only SVGs: exact original path geometry, physical dimensions, editable vectors; suitable source for supplier preflight.
- Typography SVGs: editable design masters, NOT outlined or colour-separated production files. Font metrics must be checked with the specified fonts before final sizing.
- Mockup PNGs: concept photography generated using the built-in image-generation tool. Shapes, lettering, colours, materials and scale may vary; SVGs/specifications govern the design. Generated captions asserting an “exact” symbol do not constitute validation.
- Screen print starting targets: positive lines ≥0.5mm, open gaps ≥0.7mm. Serif hairlines need inspection at actual size. Embroidery starting targets: strokes/gaps about 1.2–1.5mm, lettering 5–6mm high. Supplier capabilities and sew-outs govern final limits.
- Embroidery machine files, garment tech packs, physical colour matches and final printer separations are NOT supplied. No manufacturing quote or material certification is claimed.
- The original wordmark is available only as a PNG in this workspace. For final wordmark labels/packaging, obtain its original vector file; do not auto-trace the generated lookbook lettering.

## Art direction judgment

Launch the quiet tee, cap and credits hoodie, with the one-ink editorial tote as the accessory. This combines recognisable branding with a phrase people can wear without feeling like an advertisement. Keep the huge-F social tee as a limited option: recognisable, but a more demanding wear. Peach and mint work best on accessories or single supporting details. Avoid buying multiple blank colours and decoration methods in the first run; every extra position, colour, digitisation and stock variant increases cost.
