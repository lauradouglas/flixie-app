# Flixie product marketing context

**Document version:** v3
**Last updated:** 2026-10-15
**Status:** Working brief. Confirmed user facts are distinguished from hypotheses.

## Product overview
Flixie is a consumer movie and TV discovery, tracking and social app. Members can maintain watchlists, log viewing, rate/review, track TV progress, exchange recommendations and plan watches with friends. It does not stream films or episodes. User confirms app, corporate website and Instagram are live. Verify public-release availability before advertising a feature; local code and TestFlight are not proof of a public release. Business model and ongoing pricing need confirmation.

## Target audience
**User-selected first audience:** Film and TV enthusiasts who love tracking and reviewing.
**Geography/language:** English-speaking audiences worldwide, confirmed by founder. Age range and narrower communities remain open. Use clear English understandable internationally; localise provider/availability claims by region. B2B personas do not apply.

## Goals and resources
User goals: downloads, sign-ups and people coming back. Approximately £20/month marketing budget; minimise spending. **Founder time budget:** 30 minutes per week total for marketing. Assistant prepares research, ideas, drafts and creative assets; keep the founder’s review and engagement tasks within this allowance. Do not prescribe daily manual work or assume unattended publishing is configured. Instagram is the starting channel. Promotion had not started because the founder was waiting for App Store availability and reviews. No numerical growth targets agreed. No paid tools, ads, messaging, scheduling or production changes are authorised by this brief.

## Positioning and problems
**User-desired outcome:** People can find things to watch, trust that a choice is a good pick and share it with friends. **Working positioning for review:** Find your next watch through friends you trust. Make friend recommendations concrete in demos, including recommend/not-recommend signals. Preserve solo discovery/tracking value for new members before friends join.

Working themes from existing public copy: keep a viewing diary, remember saved titles, discover through friends and turn recommendations into plans. These are messaging hypotheses, not validated customer research. Prior assistant suggestion focused on friends/couples choosing a film; the user has since selected enthusiasts who track and review as the first audience. Revisit future content accordingly.

## Competitive landscape and differentiation
**Founder-confirmed value:** The founder cares more about friends’ opinions than opinions from people they do not know. Flixie shows what friends think and whether they recommend a title or not. Lead with trusted friend opinions helping people find a good pick and share it with friends. This is the founder’s product rationale, not a claim that every friend recommendation guarantees enjoyment or that competitors lack this capability. Named competitor comparisons and switching reasons remain unvalidated. Do not invent competitor deficiencies or uniqueness claims. Do not claim import/migration capability without verification.

## Objections and switching dynamics
Research pending. Possible questions to investigate include starting another diary, bringing existing history and getting value before friends join. These are assistant hypotheses, not customer quotes. No testimonials, proven retention figures or conversion attribution are established.

## Voice and customer language
**Confirmed personality:** Enthusiastic but chill, with jokes; never a know-it-all. Founder wording: “I like an enthusiastic but like chill not likea no it all with jokes”. Write like a relaxed friend who loves films and TV: curious, welcoming and playful. Share opinions as personal taste rather than authoritative verdicts. Avoid gatekeeping, superiority, lecturing and forcing a joke into every line. Their own launch caption includes “For the overflowing watchlist, the unfinished series and the group chat that can never pick a film. 🥺🍿” and “links in da bio.” Treat these as founder voice examples, not customer testimonials. Conversational British English is a working choice. Founder story (verbatim): “I made flixie cause I love movies, I wanted to be able to plan movie sessions easier, and see watch providers easier and find stuff to watch easily”. Founder motivations: love of movies, easier planning, clearer provider discovery and finding something to watch. Do not recast this as a complaint about a named competitor.

## Founder taste and editorial starting points
**User favourites:** Interstellar, Se7en, Alien; Hunter × Hunter, Attack on Titan (AoT), Succession. Cover films, live-action TV and anime. These titles establish founder taste, not a restriction on the account or proof of audience preferences. Assistant interpretation: potential starting themes include sci-fi, tense thrillers, character drama and anime; test rather than assume performance. Keep plot twists and anime outcomes spoiler-safe.
