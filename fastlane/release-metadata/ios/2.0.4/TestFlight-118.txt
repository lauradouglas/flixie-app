Cinema notebook reviews
- Choose “Write a review next” while logging a watch, then continue to the full review editor after saving.
- Write a separate title and review on lavender notebook paper, with Pip, compact gold ratings and a Post review button that stays above the keyboard.
- Keep your rating, recommendation, watch link and spoiler preference when continuing or editing.
- Cancelling a review keeps your watch saved.
