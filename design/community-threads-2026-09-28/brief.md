# Community discussion thread
Mode: Operate / Read. Mobile iOS and Android concept, extending the approved Anime community visual system. Manrope, dark purple, mint spoiler-free metadata, light purple mentions, subtle dividers, personal badge borders.

Keep the original post distinct from the conversation. Flat chronological comments with a short, tappable parent reference; no deep indentation on a small phone. A persistent reply composer keeps the target visible and can cancel it without losing text. Mention suggestions show why someone is eligible: friend in this community or participant in this thread. Joining is required to reply, not to read. Spoiler boundaries hide both post content and replies until explicitly revealed.

Deliverable is a local interactive mockup, not Flutter implementation. Fictional users and messages. Preview states: discussion, targeted reply, mention selection, spoiler gate, visitor, send failure/retry. No actual notifications or posts. No new feature approval inferred.
