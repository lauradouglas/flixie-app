I’d change the order and amount of content before adding more features. Home should help you resume, choose, or reconnect.

Keep plans when they matter

Show your next plan or a decision waiting on you. Replace the permanent empty-plan promotion with a smaller contextual invitation when there’s nothing scheduled.

Move personal content above Trending

Just for you and Continue Watching deserve earlier space. Use compact poster rows rather than consecutive large carousels.

Add “From your watchlist”

Resurface three saved films to help people act on what they already liked. No availability claim without provider data. If the watchlist is empty, omit this section.

Add one community conversation

A spoiler-free prompt from a joined community gives people a reason to return. One thread, then “View community”. This needs a new Home query and existing safety rules.

Trim repeated shortcuts and activity

Search and Watchlist already live in the bottom navigation. Keep Invite in Social and plan actions with plans. Preserve Around Flixie, but show one useful preview with a route to the full feed.

Keep your picker lower down

“Find tonight’s film” stays after personal discovery, exactly as you requested. Trending becomes a compact section near the bottom, rather than Home’s main event.

Content rules & implementation scope

Hide empty sections. Don’t invent friends, activity or personalised claims for new users. Skip Continue Watching when there’s nothing underway. Never expose spoiler text in previews.

  1. Existing: plans, recommendations, continuing series, picker, public activity.
  2. Small change: reorder sections, compact cards, remove duplicate shortcuts.
  3. New wiring: saved-film resurfacing and joined-community discussion preview.
