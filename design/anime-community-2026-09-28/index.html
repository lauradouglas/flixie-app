Anime films and series, together. Enthusiastic takes, thoughtful criticism and new favourites.

How community ratings work

Scores come from joined members’ public reviews, with one latest score per person per title. Low ratings count too. These are Anime community scores, not global Flixie ratings.

Privacy & spoilers

Joining never makes a private review public. Leaving removes your reviews here. Spoiler content stays hidden until revealed. Your existing rating visibility preferences still apply.

Keep it welcoming

Disagree with the take, respect the person. Mark spoilers. Report a review or block someone from the original post.

Why this community would be useful

This is a design proposal, not a live community. All people, reviews, counts and scores above are illustrative; the title artwork is from TMDB. The app’s Manrope typography, purple surfaces, mint accents and personal badge borders are retained.

  • First, give me something worth watching. Member scores, sample sizes, film/series labels and quick watchlist actions make the feed useful even before someone joins.
  • Then give me people worth listening to. Put taste and actual reviews ahead of follower counts, streaks or a popularity leaderboard. Low-scoring reviews belong here too.
  • Give me a reason to return. Discovery and discussion receive equal prominence, as requested. Start with useful recommendation questions and title-specific threads, with spoiler boundaries visible before opening. Keep original review comments alongside their reviews.
  • Already supported: explicit join/leave, public member reviews, Latest/Popular, film and series in Anime, per-title member scores, original review destinations, personal badges and spoiler controls.
  • Proposed additions: a Discuss feed, title/episode-scoped threads, replies, a Discover collection, films/series filter, member browsing, contextual watchlist actions and a curated starting point. These require integration and, for collections and member discovery, new queries. All prototype actions are local only.
  • Later, if activity earns it: optional community watch-alongs and discussion notifications with explicit opt-in. No fake “online now”, empty chat rooms or automatic notifications.

Try Join, Discuss threads, a local reply, Start a discussion, the tabs, filters, sort, Watchlist and spoiler reveal. The lower app navigation is shown for placement only. Series artwork is intentionally a labelled placeholder.
