The Episodes tab should make two things easy: choose a season and record what you’ve watched. Each gets one clear control.

Smaller season posters

Keep artwork, with season names and episode counts beneath it. An underline marks the selected season. Remove checkmarks from posters so selection can’t be mistaken for completion.

One season selector

The poster strip already selects the season, so the second dropdown disappears. The active season’s name and watched count sit directly above its episodes.

Simple rows, clear checkboxes

Keep episode stills and let full titles wrap. Remove nested cards, truncated plot snippets and the extra chevron. One square checkbox records watched status; tapping the title opens details.

Say what the action does

Replace “Watch all” with “Mark season watched”. It updates your record, so confirm the bulk change and offer Undo. Keep future episodes out of a bulk watched action.

Less clutter, fewer spoilers

Show episode number and runtime in the list. Keep the synopsis and original air date in episode details. Give unaired episodes a clear upcoming date.
