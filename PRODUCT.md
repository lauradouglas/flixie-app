# Flixie

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
adaptive

## Product Purpose
Flixie helps people discover films and shows, collect a watchlist, track viewing,
and choose what to watch with friends. Product history lives in docs/product-ideas.md.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing Flutter application with feature-owned state and API access. Native iOS
and Android; the iOS release also runs on supported Macs. Local work does not
authorise a production deployment. User requested Pip’s Popcorn Catch with a
leaderboard and approved a cinema arcade direction.

## Brand Commitments
Use the official F for compact branding and the Flixie wordmark: white flix,
purple ie on dark surfaces. Pip is the established purple popcorn-haired mascot.
Preserve each user's own earned avatar border. Confirmations use bottom sheets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive phones, tablets, landscape and increased text scaling. Preserve clear
touch targets and meaningful content. Game practice should not require timed play.
